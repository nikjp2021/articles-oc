## 🤖 What if you had a team of AI specialists doing the grunt work?

That's what we built. A multi-agent content pipeline that takes one idea and turns it into four platform-optimized articles — with real citations, image prompts, and a quality score — in about 48 minutes.

Here's how it works:

**🔍 Step 1: Research** — An AI agent scours the web for real data, trends, and statistics on your topic. Every number has a source. No made-up facts.

**✍️ Step 2: Write** — Four different AI writers create versions tailored for LinkedIn, Facebook, Threads, and Instagram. Each one sounds like it belongs on that platform.

**📁 Step 3: Organize** — Everything gets sorted into folders. No messy desktop.

**🎨 Step 4: Enrich** — Image prompts with alt text are generated for each article. Visuals that match the content.

**✅ Step 5: Review** — A reviewer scores every article against a 9-point quality rubric. Weak hook? Missing citation? It gets flagged and rewritten.

**👤 Step 6: You publish** — Here's the most important step: YOU review and approve before anything goes live. AI does the drafting. You do the deciding.
