# They Told Us Iraq Had Nukes. They Were Wrong. 20 Years Later, We're Still Paying.

🔥 Remember March 2003?

I do. I remember watching Colin Powell at the UN, holding up that vial of anthrax, telling the world that Iraq had weapons of mass destruction. I remember the speeches and the "shock and awe" on TV.

And I remember the silence when we found out there was nothing there. 😶

Not a single stockpile. No active program. The UN inspectors said it. The CIA later admitted it. The 1,400-person Iraq Survey Group spent years searching and came up empty.

**The cost was devastating:**
🔹 **$1.1 trillion** in direct U.S. spending alone.
🔹 **4,400+ American lives.** Gone.
🔹 **187,000+ Iraqi civilians.** Dead.

All based on intelligence that the Senate Intelligence Committee later described as "unreliable sources" producing "false or exaggerated allegations." 💔

### **The Hypocrisy We Ignore** 🧐

Before Saddam was our enemy, he was our ally. During the 1980s, the U.S. provided intelligence to Iraq — fully aware, per declassified CIA docs, that Saddam would use it to launch chemical weapons attacks. Mustard gas. Sarin.

The U.S. knew. And did it anyway.

By 2003, the same man the West had helped arm was the reason we were going to war. The "existential threat" was, in part, one we had helped create. 🤔

### **The Nuclear Double Standard** ☢️

The Treaty on the Non-Proliferation of Nuclear Weapons (NPT) promised that nuclear nations would disarm while others wouldn't build nukes.

Here we are, 2026:
🚫 The U.S. is spending **$1.7 trillion** to modernize its entire nuclear arsenal (Arms Control Association, May 2026).
🚫 Russia and the U.S. still hold **~90% of all nuclear weapons** — about 12,241 warheads.
🚫 Israel has an estimated **90 nukes** — and the U.S. officially refuses to even acknowledge it.

We tell Iran and North Korea: "You can't have nukes."
We tell the world: "Trust us." **But why should they?**

### **Who Protects Us?** 🛡️

Global military spending hit **$2.89 trillion in 2025** (SIPRI). The U.S. accounts for **$954 billion** — more than the next nine countries combined. 💸

When intelligence fails and thousands die — who is held accountable? The architects of the Iraq War moved on, wrote memoirs, and kept their power.

Who protects us from the protectors? 💬
