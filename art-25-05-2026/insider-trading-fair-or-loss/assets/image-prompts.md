# Image Prompts for Insider Trading Fair or Loss Articles

## LinkedIn Article Image Prompt
**Prompt:** Professional split-screen illustration showing left side: shadowy figures exchanging documents with downward trending stock chart, right side: transparent blockchain-style network with upward trending chart and diverse group of professionals analyzing data. Color scheme: dark blues and grays on left transitioning to greens and golds on right. Style: Modern infographic meets editorial illustration.
**Alt Text:** Split visual contrasting illicit insider trading (shadowy document exchange, falling stocks) with ethical market analysis (transparent network, rising charts, diverse professionals).

## Facebook Article Image Prompt
**Prompt:** Friendly, approachable illustration of two people having coffee at a cafe, one pointing at a laptop showing stock charts with concerned expression, the other explaining with hand gestures. Background shows typical street cafe with plants and warm lighting. Style: Warm, flat-design illustration with soft corners and inviting color palette.
**Alt Text:** Two people discussing stock market concerns over coffee at a cafe - one showing worried expression at laptop charts, other explaining concepts with friendly hand gestures.

## Threads/X Article Image Prompt
**Prompt:** Minimalist line art style illustration of a scale tipping slightly - one side labeled "Information Advantage" with briefcase and laptop icons, other side labeled "Market Fairness" with group of people icons. Simple black lines on white background with one accent color (red) on the tipping point.
**Alt Text:** Simple line drawing of a balance scale slightly tipping, showing tension between information advantage (briefcase/laptop) and market fairness (group of people).

## Instagram Carousel Slide Prompts
**Slide 1 (Hook):** Bold text overlay on blurred background of stock exchange floor with golden particles floating in air. Text: "Is Insider Trading Fair or Foul?"
**Alt Text:** Blurred stock exchange background with golden floating particles, overlay text asking "Is Insider Trading Fair or Foul?"

**Slide 2 (Definition):** Simple icon of eye with slash over it (representing non-public info) beside trading chart icon, connected by arrows showing flow from information to trading action.
**Alt Text:** Icons showing blocked eye (non-public information) connected via arrows to trading chart, illustrating insider trading definition.

**Slide 3 (Unfair Argument):** Split image - left side: frustrated diverse group of people looking at falling graphs, right side: small group celebrating with rising graphs and money symbols.
**Alt Text:** Split visualization showing frustrated majority vs. celebrating few in insider trading scenario.

**Slide 4 (Efficient Argument):** Animation-style frame showing fast-forward symbol over stock chart that quickly adjusts to new information level, with "Price Discovery" label.
**Alt Text:** Fast-forward symbol over rapidly adjusting stock chart illustrating price discovery concept.

**Slide 5 (Recent Cases):** Timeline graphic with three markers: Dec 2025 ($600K scheme), Jun 2025 (CEO case), May 2026 (21-person SEC charge).
**Alt Text:** Timeline showing three recent insider trading cases with dates and descriptions.

**Slide 6 (Fairness Factors):** Four quadrants showing: 1) Legal vs Illegal info source, 2) Fiduciary duty scale, 3) Intent spectrum, 4) Impact measurement.
**Alt Text:** Four-quadrant diagram illustrating factors that determine insider trading fairness.

**Slide 7 (Regulator Response):** Illustration of SEC building facade with magnifying glass examining documents showing charts and dollar signs, with arrows pointing to enforcement actions.
**Alt Text:** SEC building with magnifying glass examining financial documents, showing regulatory scrutiny focus.

**Slide 8 (Professional Guidelines):** Three-step visual: 1) Check policy book, 2) Pause symbol over trade button, 3) Long-term growth chart.
**Alt Text:** Three-step guide for professionals: check company policies, avoid trading when unsure, focus on long-term value.

**Slide 9 (Question):** Bold text on gradient background: "Your Turn: Is there ANY scenario where insider trading helps markets?" with chat bubble icons.
**Alt Text:** Gradient background with bold question about insider trading benefits and chat bubble icons inviting responses.

**Slide 10 (CTA):** Save icon with text "Save for ethics discussion" and comment bubble asking "What's ONE thing companies could do better?"
**Alt Text:** Save button icon with invitation to save for ethics discussion and comment prompt about corporate improvements.
