## **Caption:**

Is Gen AI at work smart… or is it cheating? 🐘

The calculator analogy is perfect. Nobody calls a calculator "cheating" for your personal budget. But use it in a "no-calculator" math test? That’s cheating.

The difference isn’t the tool. It’s the context and the expectations.

Using AI to brainstorm, draft, and research is working smarter. But using it to replace your judgment while lying about your involvement is cheating.

A 2026 study by Novoresume found that 1 in 7 people landed their job by having AI whisper answers during interviews. This isn't about being anti-AI—it's about being clear on what makes YOU valuable.

Your value isn’t how many words you can type. It’s your judgment, experience, and accountability. None of which AI can replace.
