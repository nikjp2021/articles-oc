# Logical qubits just beat physical qubits at solving differential equations 😱

1/ This changes everything we thought we knew about quantum error correction. 🔬  

Pasqal demonstrated on May 21, 2026 that their logical qubits (using extra physical qubits for error detection) actually OUTPERFORMED raw physical qubits when solving real-world differential equations. 🚀

The stats are wild: Over 50% better accuracy on average (Pasqal test results, May 2026), and up to 10x gains on nonlinear problems. 📈

And this happened despite the logical approach being MORE complex. How? 🧵👇

2/ They used a [[4,2,2]] quantum error-detecting code on their 99.4% fidelity neutral-atom processor. ⚡  

The trick: logical qubits are specially tuned to catch **coherent errors**—those predictable phase shifts that build up over time and ruin differential equation solutions. 🎯

Standard physical qubits? They’re blind to these. 🙈
Logical encoding? Acts like a high-precision noise filter. 🛡️

3/ This wasn’t just a toy subroutine test. 📊  

They ran a full quantum kernel algorithm across **1,000 real differential equations** from fluid dynamics, weather modeling, and financial systems (per Pasqal's May 2026 benchmark). 🌍

Logical qubits won every time. 🏆
Median residual error dropped from 0.069 down to 0.042. 📉
On a tough nonlinear case? Error went from 0.122 down to 0.011! 🤯

4/ Why does this matter? 💡  

For years we saw error correction as a costly overhead—more qubits, more gates, more delay. A "tax" on performance. 💸

Pasqal’s result shows it can be a **PERFORMANCE BOOSTER** when matched to the right algorithm. 🏎️
Fault-tolerant quantum computing isn’t just for the distant future; it’s delivering advantages NOW on near-term hardware. ⏳

5/ The big takeaway: stop thinking of logical qubits as just error correction. 🎯  

Think of them as **application-specific enhancers.** ✨

By running complete end-to-end workflows (not just isolated gates), Pasqal pinpointed exactly which noise sources matter for practical problems—giving them a roadmap for better hardware. 🗺️
