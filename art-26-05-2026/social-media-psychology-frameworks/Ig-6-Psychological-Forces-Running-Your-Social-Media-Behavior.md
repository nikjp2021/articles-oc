📊 SOCIAL COMPARISON THEORY

Meta-analysis of 94 studies (N=36,583): upward comparison linked to psychological maladjustment (r=0.33). Strongest link — social-evaluative negative emotions (r=0.438).

**Your brain evolved to compare. Social media gave you 24/7 access.**

📡 THE INTEREST GRAPH SHIFT

Shift from Social Graph (who you follow) to Interest Graph (what you watch). Mental Momentum Research (May 2026): "The interest graph decouples reach from follower counts." Most of your feed now comes from people you don't follow.

**You're an attention vector being optimized for dwell time.**
