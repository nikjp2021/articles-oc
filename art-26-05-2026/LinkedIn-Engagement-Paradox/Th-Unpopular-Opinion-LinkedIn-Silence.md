# 🔥 unpopular opinion: your linkedin connections aren't ignoring you — they're scared of you

![Raw high-contrast close-up of a frustrated professional staring at a laptop screen with LinkedIn analytics showing near-zero engagement, face lit only by screen glow.](th-harsh-reality.jpg)

ok let's talk about the elephant in the feed.

![Macro close-up of a phone screen showing a LinkedIn post with only 2 likes, with a small brass elephant figurine in the background representing the unspoken issue.](th-elephant-in-feed.jpg)

you spend 2 hours crafting a post. publish. crickets.

next day you repost a stranger's identical take. 87 likes. 14 comments. from YOUR network.

what the actual hell.

i dug into the psychology. here's what's actually happening 👇

---

1/ SOCIAL COMPARISON IS AUTOMATIC

every time a connection sees your post, their brain runs a comparison. "they got promoted. they're speaking at conferences."

a 2026 study found daily peer-achievement viewing leads to 34% lower job satisfaction (sheikh, 2026).

engaging with you = publicly acknowledging your success WHILE comparing their career to yours. heavy.

with a stranger? zero comparison cost. 💯

---

2/ LINKEDIN GIVES EVERYONE IMPOSTER SYNDROME

researchers proved just *using* linkedin triggers imposter thoughts (marder et al., 2025). your connection's brain whispers: "if i engage, will they check my profile? will they judge me?"

so they don't engage. it's safer.

a stranger has no shared history. no evaluation threat. easy engage. 🎯

---

3/ THE "FRIEND vs MARKETER" TRAP

research in Information Systems Frontiers (2026) calls this the "dual-role trust model." engaging with you means signaling: "i endorse your standing" AND "i accept the reciprocal expectation."

that's heavy.

with a stranger: "i found this useful." done. no baggage. ⚡

---

4/ THE ALGORITHM ISN'T HELPING

linkedin's 360Brew AI shifted to an Interest Graph. only 31% of your feed comes from people you know (goodman, 2026).

your network literally sees less of you. AND when they do see you, the psychology works against you. double whammy.

---

5/ FEAR OF NEGATIVE EVALUATION IS THE ROOT

![High-contrast macro of an anatomical brain model with glowing red neural pathways highlighting the fear of negative evaluation as the core symptom node.](th-fne.jpg)

a 2026 network analysis found fear of negative evaluation (FNE) is the #1 driver of social avoidance online (tuerxun & zou, 2026).

your connection isn't ignoring you. they're avoiding the discomfort of being compared to you.

it's not personal. it's neurobiology. 🧬

![First-person close-up of hands hovering over a laptop keyboard, hesitating to type a comment, with only the screen light illuminating the scene.](th-hesitation.jpg)

---

so what do you do?

stop taking it personally. write for the stranger who shares your interest, not the connection who knows your name. lower the threshold — give people an A/B/C choice. build topic authority.

the goal isn't to get your connections to engage.

the goal is to stop needing them to.

thoughts? am i wrong? have you felt this? 👇
