# Your AI Content Pipeline, Visualized: From Idea to 4 Published Articles

**Carousel Text:**

**Slide 1 (Hook):**
🤯 You have a content idea. What if a team of AI agents could turn it into 4 platform-ready articles — with citations, image prompts, and quality scoring — in under an hour?

**Slide 2 (The Problem):**
⏳ The Old Way:
1. Research (1-2 hrs)
2. Write LinkedIn version (1 hr)
3. Rewrite for Facebook (45 min)
4. Rewrite for Threads (30 min)
5. Rewrite for Instagram (45 min)
6. Find/create images (1 hr)
7. Quality check (30 min)
= ~6 hours per topic

**Slide 3 (The Solution):**
🤖 The Multi-Agent Pipeline:
No single AI does everything. Each agent is a specialist:

🔍 Researcher → Finds real citations via web search
✍️ Writer → Creates 4 platform-specific drafts
📁 Organizer → Files into clean folder structure
🎨 Enricher → Generates image prompts + alt text
⭐ Reviewer → Scores against 9-criteria rubric

A 2025 MDPI study confirmed that platform-specific AI content performs measurably better than generic output (Ahn & Kim, *Electronics* 14(21), 2025).

= ~48 minutes of AI work

**Slide 4 (The Flow):**
📈 Pipeline Flow:
Idea → Research Brief → 4 Articles Written → Organized → Enriched with Visuals → Reviewed & Scored → HUMAN reviews → Published

Each stage passes structured artifacts to the next. No single point of failure.

**Slide 5 (The Rubric):**
⭐ 9-Criteria Quality Score:
Each article scored 1-5 on:
• Hook Strength (×2 weight)
• Citation Quality (×2)
• Virality Potential (×2)
• Platform Tone Fit
• Tone Differentiation
• Emoji Strategy
• Structure & Flow
• Length Appropriateness
• Visual Formatting

Score = (Points ÷ 60) × 5

Failing articles get auto-flagged for rewrite.

**Slide 6 (The Human Gate):**
🚪 The Human-In-The-Loop:
AI does the drafting. AI enriches. AI reviews.

But the FINAL CHECKPOINT is always human:
✅ Verify citations
✅ Check for hallucinations
✅ Approve or reject narratives
✅ Hit publish

This isn't about replacement. It's about empowerment.

**Slide 7 (The Result):**
🎯 From idea to 4 polished articles + image assets + quality scores in ~48 minutes.

You get 7 hours back. Your content quality stays high. Your voice stays authentic.
